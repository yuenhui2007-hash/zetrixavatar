Level 4 of 6

AI for Daily Work

Travel planning, meal planning, budgeting, learning, writing, scheduling, and shopping. Estimated time: 2 hours.

Module 1: Travel Planning

How AI Helps

  • Itinerary creation: "Plan a 5-day trip to Tokyo for £800 budget. Include cultural sites, food experiences, and one day trip."
  • Flight/hotel comparison: Upload options; AI analyses value, location, reviews
  • Packing lists: "What should I pack for a business trip to Dubai in July?"
  • Language prep: "Teach me 20 essential Japanese phrases for tourists"
  • Local customs: "What cultural etiquette should I know for South Korea?"

🎯 Challenge

Use AI to plan a weekend trip within 2 hours of your home. Include: transport, accommodation, 3 activities, restaurant recommendations, and estimated budget.

Module 2: Budgeting & Finance

Personal Finance with AI

  • Budget creation: "Create a monthly budget for £2500 income with savings goal of £500"
  • Expense tracking: Upload bank statements; AI categorises spending
  • Debt planning: "What's the fastest way to pay off £5000 credit card debt at 19% APR?"
  • Investment basics: "Explain index funds vs ETFs in simple terms"
  • Savings goals: "How long to save £10,000 at £300/month with 4% interest?"

💡 Pro Tip

Upload a CSV of your monthly expenses. Ask AI to identify patterns and suggest 3 areas to cut costs.

Module 3: Learning & Skill Building

Accelerate Learning

  • Study plans: "Create a 12-week plan to learn Python from scratch"
  • Explanation: "Explain quantum computing like I'm 10"
  • Flashcards: "Generate 20 flashcards for GCSE Biology cell structure"
  • Practice questions: "Give me 5 calculus integration problems with solutions"
  • Language learning: "Correct my Spanish: 'Yo tener hambre'"

Research Assistance

  • Summarise academic papers in plain English
  • Compare theories or methodologies
  • Find gaps in your knowledge: "What topics should I know before studying machine learning?"

Module 4: Writing & Communication

Everyday Writing Tasks

  • Emails: Professional, casual, apology, follow-up, introduction
  • Messages: "Rewrite this more diplomatically: [text]"
  • Social media: Captions, LinkedIn posts, tweets
  • Creative: Stories, poems, song lyrics, speeches
  • Editing: "Check this for grammar, tone, and clarity"

🎯 Challenge

Write a difficult email you've been avoiding. Use AI to draft it, then refine it yourself. Send it.

Module 5: Health & Scheduling

Health Assistance

  • Meal planning: "Plan a week's meals: high protein, vegetarian, £40 budget"
  • Workout plans: "Create a 20-minute home workout for beginners, no equipment"
  • Sleep hygiene: "How can I improve my sleep quality?"
  • Reminder systems: "Create a medication reminder schedule"

Note: AI is not a doctor. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice.

Scheduling

  • Time blocking: "Plan my Saturday: gym, grocery, study, social, rest"
  • Meeting scheduling: "Draft an email to schedule a team meeting next week"
  • Deadline management: "I have 3 projects due in 2 weeks. Create a daily schedule."

📋 Final Challenge

Use AI to plan either: (A) a weekend trip with full itinerary and budget, OR (B) a monthly personal budget with savings goals. Submit your AI conversation screenshots.
