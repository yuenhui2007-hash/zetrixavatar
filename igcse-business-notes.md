# IGCSE Business Studies Notes (0450)

## 1. UNDERSTANDING BUSINESS ACTIVITY

### What is Business?
> An organization engaged in commercial, industrial, or professional activities to produce goods or services.

### Needs vs Wants

| Needs | Wants |
|-------|-------|
| Essential for survival | Desires beyond basic needs |
| Food, water, shelter, clothing | Luxury items, entertainment |
| Limited | Unlimited |

### Factors of Production

| Factor | Description | Reward |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Land** | Natural resources | Rent |
| **Labour** | Human effort | Wages |
| **Capital** | Man-made tools, machinery | Interest |
| **Enterprise** | Risk-taking, organizing | Profit |

### Sectors of Industry

| Sector | Activity | Examples |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Primary** | Extract raw materials | Farming, mining, fishing |
| **Secondary** | Manufacture goods | Car factory, baker |
| **Tertiary** | Provide services | Retail, banking, transport |
| **Quaternary** | Information/knowledge | Research, IT, consulting |

### Business Growth
- **Internal/Organic:** Reinvest profits, new products, new markets
- **External:** Mergers, takeovers, joint ventures

### Types of Business Ownership

| Type | Ownership | Liability | Control | Examples |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Sole Trader** | One person | Unlimited | Full | Local shop |
| **Partnership** | 2–20 people | Unlimited | Shared | Law firm |
| **Private Limited (Ltd)** | Shareholders | Limited | Directors | Family firm |
| **Public Limited (PLC)** | Public | Limited | Board | Multinational |
| **Cooperative** | Members | Limited | Members | Retail co-op |
| **Franchise** | Franchisee | Limited | Franchisor sets rules | McDonald's |
| **Joint Venture** | Two+ businesses | Shared | Shared | Specific project |

### Ltd vs PLC

| Feature | Ltd | PLC |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Shares sold to public | No | Yes |
| Shares on stock exchange | No | Yes |
| Minimum shareholders | 1 | 2 |
| Minimum share capital | £1 | £50,000 |
| Disclosure | Less | More (annual reports) |

## 6. FINANCIAL INFORMATION

### Sources of Finance

| Short-Term | Medium/Long-Term |
|------------|------------------|
| Overdraft | Bank loan |
| Trade credit | Share capital |
| Debt factoring | Debentures |
| Leasing | Retained profit |
| | Mortgage |
| | Venture capital |
| | Government grants |

**Internal:** Retained profit, sale of assets
**External:** Debt, equity, grants

### Cash Flow
- **Cash inflows:** Sales, loans, investments
- **Cash outflows:** Wages, materials, rent, tax
- **Net cash flow:** Inflows - Outflows
- **Opening balance + Net cash flow = Closing balance**

### Income Statement (Simplified)
```
Revenue (Sales)
- Cost of sales
= Gross profit
- Expenses (wages, rent, marketing)
= Net profit (profit before tax)
- Tax
= Profit after tax
```

### Key Ratios

| Ratio | Formula | What It Shows |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Gross profit margin** | (Gross profit / Revenue) × 100 | Profit from sales |
| **Net profit margin** | (Net profit / Revenue) × 100 | Overall profitability |
| **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)** | (Net profit / Capital employed) × 100 | Efficiency of capital |
| **Current ratio** | Current assets / Current liabilities | Short-term liquidity |
| **Acid test** | (Current assets - Stock) / Current liabilities | Immediate liquidity |

### Break-Even Analysis
```
Break-even (units) = Fixed costs / (Selling price - Variable cost per unit)
Contribution per unit = Selling price - Variable cost per unit
Margin of safety = Actual sales - Break-even sales
```

## 7. HUMAN RESOURCES

### Recruitment Process
1. Job analysis → Job description + Person specification
2. Advertise vacancy (internal/external)
3. Receive applications/CVs
4. Shortlist candidates
5. Interview/assessment
6. Select and appoint
7. Induction/training

### Job Description vs Person Specification

| Job Description | Person Specification |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| What the job involves | What the ideal candidate needs |
| Tasks, responsibilities | Skills, qualifications, experience |
| Working conditions | Personal qualities |

### Training Methods

| On-the-Job | Off-the-Job |
|------------|-------------|
| Coaching, mentoring, shadowing | Courses, workshops, online |
| Cheaper, immediate | Broader skills, networking |
| May learn bad habits | Time away from work |

- Workers motivated by money
- Time-and-motion studies
- Piece-rate pay

**Maslow (Hierarchy of Needs):**

## EXAM TIPS

1. **Use business terminology** — Not "money" but "revenue," "profit," "capital"
2. **Apply to case study** — Use the business name, refer to specific details
3. **Explain "why"** — Not just what but why it matters
4. **Consider stakeholders** — Different groups affected differently
5. **Quantitative analysis** — Calculate ratios, break-even, percentages
6. **Advantages AND disadvantages** — Balanced argument
7. **Recommendations** — State what should be done and justify
8. **Short-term vs long-term** — Consider time frame
9. **Read command words** — Identify, explain, analyze, evaluate, recommend
10. **Use frameworks** — PESTLE, SWOT, Ansoff where relevant

### Common Calculation Checklist
- Break-even: Fixed costs / Contribution per unit
- Profit margins: (Profit / Revenue) × 100
- ROCE: (Profit / Capital employed) × 100
- Ratios: Current ratio, acid test
- Market share: (Firm / Total) × 100
- Exchange rate conversions
- Percentage changes
