Module 1: What is Claw?

Claw is Zetrix AI's interactive avatar system. Unlike text-only AI, Claw engages through:

  • Voice conversations: Natural spoken dialogue
  • Visual presence: Animated avatar for presentations
  • Scenario roleplay: Practice difficult conversations
  • Meeting mode: AI participant that takes notes and answers questions

When to Use Claw vs Text Chat

Use Claw For Use Text Chat For
Practice presentations Quick factual queries
Roleplay interviews Document analysis
Customer service training Code debugging
Meeting participation Data analysis

Module 2: Voice Conversations

Getting Started

  • Click the microphone icon to start voice mode
  • Speak naturally — Claw understands context and follow-ups
  • Use "Hey Claw" wake word for hands-free activation
  • Interrupt anytime — Claw adapts to your pace

Voice Commands

  • "Claw, summarise this document"
  • "Switch to presentation mode"
  • "Save this to my notes"
  • "Roleplay: You're an angry customer"
  • " slower please" / "Explain that again"

🎯 Practice Task

Start a voice conversation with Claw. Ask it to explain a complex topic from your work. Practice interrupting and asking follow-up questions.

Module 3: Roleplay Scenarios

Claw can simulate realistic scenarios for practice. You cannot skip — must complete the conversation.

🎯 Customer Complaint

Claw plays an angry customer who received a damaged product. Your goal: de-escalate, apologise, and offer a solution.

🎯 Sales Pitch

Claw is a sceptical prospect. Practice handling objections and closing the deal.

🎯 Interview Practice

Claw is the hiring manager. Answer behavioural and technical questions. Get instant feedback on your responses.

🎯 Performance Review

Practice giving constructive feedback to an underperforming team member (played by Claw).

🎯 Negotiation

Claw is a vendor pushing for higher prices. Negotiate better terms while maintaining the relationship.

📋 Assignment

Complete 3 roleplay scenarios with Claw. Submit screenshots of your completed conversations.

Module 4: Presentation Mode

Using Claw for Presentations

  • Co-presenter: Claw can introduce sections or answer audience questions
  • Q&A backup: Audience asks questions; Claw provides answers on screen
  • Rehearsal: Practice your presentation; Claw gives feedback on pacing and clarity
  • Live demos: Claw can demonstrate features while you explain

Setup

  • Upload your slide deck to Claw beforehand
  • Set context: "You're co-presenting a Q3 review to the board"
  • Configure response length: "Keep answers under 30 seconds"
  • Enable visual mode for avatar display
